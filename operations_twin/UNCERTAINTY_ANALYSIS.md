# Uncertainty

Observed case-duration distribution, conditional simulation variability, document-cluster sampling uncertainty, missing service/capacity information and model inadequacy are separate.

Independent simulation replicas use NumPy SeedSequence(seed,replicate,agent,component).95%Student-t intervals quantify the mean of replica metrics;2.5–97.5%quantiles describe outcome variability. Neither interval is total epistemic uncertainty.499bootstrap resamples preserve whole purchase-document groups; shared calendar/company context can still violate exchangeability. Three nominal90%duration intervals from calibration are evaluated against actual test coverage.

What-if differences are paired by replica/case/component streams. Unchanged agents preserve route draws across demand/capacity scenarios. Changing transition weights can change later event correspondence; common seeds do not prove perfect coupling after a routing intervention. Capacity is designed from assumed service share and utilization; nine sensitivity cells show dependence on both assumptions. No interval converts an unidentifiable capacity parameter into an observed fact.
