Scientific limits

  • Experimental public-data screening, not a new DFT calculation or physical experiment.
  • TB-mBJ and optB88vdW band gaps are not pooled or treated as interchangeable.
  • Formation energy is separate from energy above hull; missing hull is excluded at the stability stage.
  • Hull threshold is exploratory and does not establish synthesis feasibility or finite-temperature stability.
  • SLME is a reported theoretical percentage, not experimental efficiency; per-record optical thickness/temperature are not encoded or independently verified.
  • Absorption spectra are not present in this bulk snapshot; dielectric diagonals are scalar descriptors, not full spectra.
  • Ranking uses transparent lexicographic order, not a learned probability or arbitrary confidence score.
  • Correlations are paired descriptive statistics, not causal laws. Source sampling and missingness limit comparisons.
  • Polymorphs retain separate JARVIS IDs; materials are not deduplicated by formula.
  • No toxicity, availability, manufacturing, environmental or economic suitability validation.
