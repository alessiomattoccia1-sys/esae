Scientific limits

  • Research only: no diagnosis, treatment or individual genetic analysis.
  • Sample is deterministic by lowest Variation ID within the official single-gene query; no representativeness claim.
  • VCV, RCV and SCV denominators are distinct. Germline classifications only.
  • Same-condition comparison requires identical NCBI-mapped MedGen condition sets; unresolved mappings are not forced.
  • P/LP and B/LB likelihood-only differences are separated from cross-band differences.
  • Contributing and noncontributing SCVs are reported separately; dates do not prove causes of disagreement.
  • VEP molecular consequence is independent of clinical classification; only exact GRCh38 SNVs qualified.
  • No artificial confidence/pathogenicity score, causal conclusion, clinical validation or model-weight learning.
