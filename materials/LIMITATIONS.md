# Scientific and implementation limits

A functioning research prototype, qualified on Mac mini M1/macOS27/Python3.12. Source setup only; no signed one-click installer and no Windows/Linux qualification. The first experiment consumes ~48MB of compressed scientific input locally; it does not run on the public VPS.

Existing database crystals selected by configurable criteria are candidates for follow-up, not new materials discovered. There is no fresh DFT simulation, physical measurement, synthesis protocol or experimentally validated device efficiency. Band gap alone does not establish absorption, transport, defect tolerance or device suitability. Geometry was validated computationally, not measured.

DFT/mBJ values have method/systematic errors. Source hull values are database-computational descriptors; thresholds do not prove finite-temperature stability or synthesizability. Formation energy is not hull energy. Negative/zero/missing fields have distinct meanings. Different polymorphs are independent JIDs.

Optical caveat:bulk per-record SLME thickness/temperature are unavailable. Two of3checked official pages give different SLME values. The fixed dataset remains the ranking source and discrepancies require follow-up; the HTML does not independently validate optical suitability. Full absorption spectra are absent. Dielectric diagonals are not full spectra.

TB-mBJcovers21,559/93,902records and SLME10,022/93,902, creating substantial method/missingness selection. The95-candidate intersection across12threshold combinations describes this dataset only. Rankings are lexicographic preferences, not probabilities, confidence scores or a scientifically optimized decision function. Correlations may reflect selection and mathematical dependence in source descriptors; no causal law is inferred.

No toxicity, economic, environmental, manufacturability or element-availability screening. No independent Materials Project comparison (connector absent). No3Dviewer; validated coordinates are inspectable locally. AI is optional, local, reference-constrained and unverified; numeric facts are formed deterministically and cannot be supplied by model prose.

Scientific source/version changes require a new study. Public normalized replay tests numerical analysis; it is not a substitute for raw acquisition/parsing verification. Dependency versions/OS outside the qualified lock may produce differing float formatting; compare outputs and investigate rather than changing expected hashes.
