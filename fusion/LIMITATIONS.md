# Limits and non-implemented extensions

v0.1 is a retrospective measurement-analysis prototype. Five MAST shots are not a representative survey of all plasma regimes. Common catalog categories do not establish identical experimental conditions. Source-relative coordinates do not establish physical phase alignment across shots. Spectral peaks and statistical excursions are not identified physical modes or discoveries.

Unknown calibration/systematic errors remain unknown. Conditional block intervals and numerical-grid widths are not total measurement uncertainty. Boxcar cell averages do not prove absence of aliasing. Correlations are descriptive; no IID significance or causal/predictive claim is made.

STFC quantitative work is limited to raw detector-frame statistics and pixel-domain method robustness. Absolute radiometry, physical plasma geometry, neutron/proton numeric interpretation and implosion-performance comparisons are not implemented. This is an explicit narrower experiment, not a claim that those diagnostics were fully analyzed.

PlasmaPy, arbitrary diagnostic plugins, LLNL simulation ingestion, persistent distributed workers and apparatus control are not implemented. Xarray/Dask lazy selected-chunk reads are implemented; this is not a claim of unlimited scale. No hard RSS cap is claimed for the Python process; bounded responses, arrays, chunk counts, ZIP expansion, native-helper resource limits and measured peaks are documented. Cancellation occurs between bounded stages, not at every CPU instruction.

Only macOS Apple Silicon/Python 3.12 is qualified. No independent physical calibration, new law, disruption predictor, confinement improvement, ignition result or hardware experiment has been established. AI is optional, local, unverified prose and cannot create measured values.
