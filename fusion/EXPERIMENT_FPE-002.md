# FPE-002 — Inertial Fusion Diagnostics

Source: STFC eData OMEAG_SAI-23A, DOI 10.5286/edata/944. The official archive is safely inventoried; seven raw detector arrays are selected by a fixed filename rule. All other contents remain inventory-only.

The output categories are ICF-01 archive/measurement inventory, ICF-02 generic pixel/intensity characterization, and ICF-03 sensitivity of the same detector measurements to six declared background/ROI combinations. The third category explicitly substitutes method robustness for cross-implosion comparison. No published ignition/performance result is claimed to have been reproduced.

Read reports/fusion/FPE-002_REPORT.html and the accompanying results, findings CSV, manifest, methodology and reproducibility instructions. See RESULTS.md for the qualified execution and remaining calibration limitations.
