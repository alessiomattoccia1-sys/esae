# Magnetic Fusion Lab

MAST is the first supported device. Browse the selected catalog metadata and diagnostic identities, choose a shot and verified signal, inspect original and filtered curves, derivatives, Welch PSD, FFT metrics and STFT. Display crop is a viewing selection, not a silent recomputation of reported study metrics.

Cross-shot tables retain each source shot ID and units. The cohort is descriptive and small; no verified common physical phase alignment is imposed. Candidate excursions are not automatically ELMs, disruptions or instabilities. Dominant frequencies are signal-processing results. Sampling, window sensitivity and source-processing caveats accompany them.

Level1 amc/plasma_current and Level2 magnetics signals have independent format adapters. No equality of their physical processing is assumed. The principal five-shot study uses Level2.
